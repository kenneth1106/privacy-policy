Account Deletion

Users can delete their Lumila account directly inside the app.

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request account deletion by emailing lumidrip.app@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will delete your account and associated data within 7 days, except for data that must be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, or financial recordkeeping purposes.

For more details on how your data is handled, please see our Privacy Policy.
